What Is A Stroll to the Stable?

A Stroll to the Stable is a free, community-built celebration of the Nativity in downtown Seguin. Hundreds of nativity scenes, lent by local families and gathered from around the world, are displayed inside the historic Nolte Building (formerly Wells Fargo) at 101 E. Nolte St. Visitors walk through at their own pace, take in the many ways people have pictured the birth of Christ, and enjoy a peaceful, reverent moment during the busy holiday season.

The event began in December 2025 as a new Seguin holiday tradition, held alongside the city's Holiday Stroll. That first year displayed more than 400 nativities, with live music from local choirs and musicians and quiet spaces for reflection. It is hosted by the City of Seguin together with churches and faith communities across the city, and it is made possible by volunteers and donations.

Common Questions

When is it?
November 27 – December 6, 2026. Saturday and Sunday 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM; Monday – Friday 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM.
Does it cost anything?
No. Admission is free and everyone is welcome.
Can I visit at a quieter time?
Additional time slots may be available by appointment for nursing homes, older adults, immunocompromised guests, and anyone who would benefit from a quieter visit or extra assistance. Email appointments@strolltothestable.com.
Can I lend my nativity?
Yes. Use Submit your Nativity on the home page to preregister, then bring it during drop-off and pick it up after the event.
Can I see the nativities online?
Yes. The Scroll to the Stable virtual tour shows nativities as they are checked in.
How can I volunteer or ask a question?
Email info@strolltothestable.com.
